HOW NIMORA WORKS

One connected system,
both sides of the roster.

Providers run operations. Support workers run their week. Nimora AI handles everything in between.

Post & match

Post a shift and Nimora matches it to verified, nearby support workers by skills, location and participant fit — you always choose who works.

Run the shift

GPS-verified clock in and out builds the timesheet automatically, while AI drafts the progress note from a quick voice or text input.

Approve & pay

Approved timesheets flow straight into pay runs — compliance logged, reminders sent, and workers paid sooner. Zero spreadsheets.
