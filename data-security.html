Data Security

Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. Our approach to security

Nimora Pty Ltd (ABN 40 624 648 266, ACN 699 396 239) takes the security of participant, support worker and provider data seriously. Given the sensitive nature of information handled on an NDIS platform, we apply layered technical and organisational safeguards across Nimora.

2. Hosting and infrastructure

Nimora data is hosted with reputable cloud infrastructure providers using industry-standard controls, including network segmentation, firewalls and regular security patching.

3. Encryption

Data transmitted to and from Nimora is encrypted in transit (via HTTPS/TLS). Sensitive data at rest, including credentials and compliance documents, is encrypted using industry-standard methods.

4. Access controls

Access to personal and sensitive information is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis, using role-based access controls and authentication safeguards.

5. Credential and compliance data

NDIS worker screening checks, police checks and other credential documents are stored securely and are only shared with the parties who need them to confirm a support worker's eligibility to work.

6. Monitoring and incident response

We monitor our systems for suspicious activity and maintain a process for responding to and reporting any data security incident, consistent with our obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Nimora service and to meet our legal and NDIS record-keeping obligations, after which it is securely deleted or de-identified.

8. Your responsibilities

You can help keep your account secure by using a strong, unique password, keeping your login details confidential, and notifying us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about our data security practices, or wish to report a security concern, please contact Nimora Pty Ltd (ABN 40 624 648 266, ACN 699 396 239) using the contact details published on our website.
